# FORMULA3

Framework for Optimal Resource Management Using Generalizable AI in FR3 Dynamic Programmable Wireless Environments

---

## Vision

FORMULA3 develops AI-native and ISAC-enabled FR3 (7–24 GHz) programmable wireless environments capable of maintaining consistent QoS under mobility, blockage, occupancy, and layout dynamics.

---

## Research Vectors

### A1: Enabling Emerging Verticals

- Multi-Gbps throughput
- Sub-millisecond latency
- Dynamic indoor and O2I connectivity
- Support for emerging 6G applications

### B1: AI-Native Design

- Physics-informed AI
- Predictive resource management
- Generalizable AI
- Continual adaptation

### B3: Integrated Sensing and Communications

- Occupancy sensing
- Mobility awareness
- Context detection
- Adaptive network control
